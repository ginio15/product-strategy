## 1. Key Observations and Trends
- **Deteriorating Profitability:** Sales volume up, yet GP, margin, EBITDA, and EBT declined versus budget. November cites “Company GP: -2.6MM or -2.7pp vs BGT,” worsening from July’s warning of being “Significantly behind Target and last Year.”
- **Sales Performance Discrepancies:** Budget performance shifted from +2% (June YTD) to −1.4% (September YTD). Technology remains strong, while Consumer Goods, Clima & Solar, and Batteries & LED Lighting lag.
- **Persistent Inventory Challenges:** July reported “4.76M stock for over 1 year”; November still notes “significant stock despite liquidation on Black Splits,” underscoring unresolved long-term stock.
- **Strategic Initiatives Progress:** PM restructuring, price management, and forecasting improvements launched but not yet reflected in financial outcomes.
- **Supplier Relations:** Toshiba negotiations unlocked YEB 2024, partially mitigating supplier risk.

## 2. Areas for Thorough Investigation

### 2.1 Worsening Profitability and Gross Margin Erosion
- **Observation:** GP, margin, EBITDA, and EBT deteriorated versus budget and LY, driven by mix shifts and destocking.
- **Red Flag:** Losses are material and unsustainable, implying deeper structural issues.
- **Key Questions:**
  - [ ] What drives the “Unfavorable Net Sales Mix,” and how will mix pivot to higher margins?
  - [ ] Beyond destocking, which pricing or cost factors cut GP and margin?
  - [ ] With the 4MM EBITDA target now unrealistic, what is the revised FY2024 EBITDA goal and path to positive EBT?
  - [ ] How do pending AUX credit notes affect Clima & Solar GP, and what is their resolution timeline?
  - [ ] When will “Public OEM Price Optimization” and “Bottom Price creation” fully lift gross margin?

### 2.2 Persistent Inventory Management Issues
- **Observation:** Significant aged stock persists despite liquidation initiatives.
- **Red Flag:** Capital and margin pressure remain high; forecasting effectiveness in doubt.
- **Key Questions:**
  - [ ] What is the current value and aging of the “significant stock,” particularly >1 year?
  - [ ] What actions ensure liquidation of the remaining €1.9M Zinc stock by April 2025 per Toshiba terms?
  - [ ] How effective were the Romania and Lenovo stock reduction plans, and what lessons emerged?
  - [ ] Which KPIs track the “LongTermStock definition” and 2025 monthly stock budget, and what targets apply?
  - [ ] Can inventory turnover by category be benchmarked versus prior periods and peers?

### 2.3 Underperforming Categories and Sales Strategy Effectiveness
- **Observation:** Batteries & LED Lighting drag GP; Clima & Solar miss GP targets; Consumer Goods mixed.
- **Red Flag:** Persistent underperformance signals strategic or execution gaps.
- **Key Questions:**
  - [ ] What root causes hamper Batteries & LED Lighting, and what recovery roadmap is in place?
  - [ ] Which concrete levers expand Batteries and Clima sales per November priorities?
  - [ ] How is profitability balanced between Public and Other channels across Tech, TV, MDA, SDA, MC?
  - [ ] What progress and results stem from Kydos rebranding, price upgrades, and >48" TV focus?
  - [ ] Where does the Praktiker–Westnet Partnership stand, and what is the hypermarket entry verdict?

### 2.4 Financial Expenses and Cash Flow Management
- **Observation:** Finance costs rising (July YTD 1,721k → November YTD 2,605k) while stock reduction remains critical.
- **Red Flag:** Higher finance costs compound negative profitability.
- **Key Questions:**
  - [ ] What drives the finance expense increase, and what controls are planned?
  - [ ] How far along is the €10M stock reduction, and how much of the €600k savings is realized or forecast?
  - [ ] What is current cash flow status given negative EBITDA/EBT, and what are near-term projections?
  - [ ] Are loan renegotiations or alternative financing considered to ease finance costs?

### 2.5 Organizational and Operational Effectiveness
- **Observation:** Structural changes (PM restructuring, sales split, new reporting) underway.
- **Red Flag:** Financials do not yet reflect operational gains; adoption risk persists.
- **Key Questions:**
  - [ ] What challenges surfaced during PM and sales reorganization, and how are they mitigated?
  - [ ] How are new Stock/Sales/Profit/Purchase reports embedded in decision-making?
  - [ ] Which KPIs gauge success of the new organization, and how is performance monitored?
  - [ ] What is the ERP tender and preparation status, and how is risk managed for rollout?
