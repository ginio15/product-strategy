# BoD Comparative Intelligence Workspace (July vs. November 2024)

## 1. Purpose & Sources
- **Objective:** Provide a single, LLM-ready knowledge base synthesizing insights from the July 23 (June YTD) and November 7 (September YTD) Board of Directors presentations to accelerate pre-meeting diligence.
- **Source Decks:** “BoD presentation 23_07_2024” and “BoD presentation 07_11_2024 v2”.
- **Current Pain Point Recap:** Holding-company teams must rapidly compare successive BoD decks to understand performance drivers, check delivery against prior commitments, detect sentiment shifts, and flag omissions. Manual review and generic LLMs are slow, error-prone, and distrustful.
- **Intended Usage:** Feed this file into downstream automation to (a) refresh metrics, (b) expand sections with direct slide citations, and (c) guide question drafting and action logging.
- **Cross-Verification Note:** All metrics and interpretations should be validated against the original PDFs once automated extraction is available. Insert slide/page references during that step.

## 3. Narrative Trend Highlights
- **Profitability Erosion:** Despite volume growth, gross margin slipped to 8.4% (−0.4pp vs. June) and remains 2.7pp below budget. EBITDA and EBT both deteriorated, underscoring structural margin pressure beyond temporary destocking.
- **Sales Mix Shift:** Technology remains the growth anchor but carries lower margin and high Public exposure. Consumer, Clima & Solar, and Batteries & Lighting trail budgets, dragging blended profitability.
- **Inventory Stagnation:** LTS remains elevated (€6.0M) with slow movement on heat pumps, zinc, and LED stock; destocking sacrifices GP without solving aging risk.
- **Finance Cost Escalation:** Interest expense rose 24% YoY; hedging covers €25M at 2.23% but leverage plus factoring dependency threaten covenant headroom.
- **Transformation Execution Lag:** Initiatives (PSI rollout, bottom prices, ERP prep, PM reorg) are “in progress,” yet KPIs, forecast accuracy, and GP improvements are unproven.
- **Vendor & Channel Dependencies:** AUX credit notes, Toshiba zinc commitments, and Public channel reliance introduce counterparty risks; evidence of restored trust is thin.

## 4. Risk & Red Flag Register

### 4.1 Profitability & Margin Integrity
- **Evidence:** GP vs. budget −€2.6M; destocking impact €424k adverse; Batteries & Lighting 32% below budget; Clima GP on legacy stock just 3.4%.
- **Implication:** FY24 revised EBITDA (€1.13M) and EBT (−€2.87M) targets appear unattainable; 2025 profitability at risk.
- **Verification Needs:** Monthly margin bridge (budget vs. actual), destocking P&L impact, revised EBITDA plan, bottom-price adherence results.

### 4.2 Inventory & Working-Capital Stress
- **Evidence:** Inventory €30.5M vs. €23.5M YE target; Romania stock reduction strides but heat pumps, zinc, LED remain; PSI only “partially live”.
- **Implication:** Cash tied up; further clearance may erode GP; YE target drift to €26.5M.
- **Verification Needs:** Category-level aging, weekly sell-out plan, PSI accuracy metrics (MAPE), inventory budget governance.

### 4.3 Vendor & Supply Dependencies
- **Evidence:** AUX rebates pending; Toshiba rebates tied to minimum zinc orders; vendor trust flagged in July; Hotpoint rollout ongoing.
- **Implication:** GP recovery contingent on external approvals; risk of forced purchases to secure rebates; reputational strain.
- **Verification Needs:** Signed vs. promised credit notes, exclusivity terms 2025+, fallback economics if rebates slip.

### 4.4 Debt, Liquidity & Financing Costs
- **Evidence:** €5.5M September loan; interest paid €2.6M YTD; factoring reliance (~€27.6M limits); equity down €3.8M in nine months.
- **Implication:** Negative EBITDA funded via debt; interest sensitivity high; covenant breach risk under downside scenarios.
- **Verification Needs:** Q4 cash forecast, covenant headroom, debt maturity ladder, hedge coverage vs. exposure.

### 4.5 Execution & Governance
- **Evidence:** July commitments (forecasting, PM empowerment, bottom prices) not translated into measurable KPIs; PM reorg adoption unclear; PSI data only partially deployed.
- **Implication:** Transformation may stall; accountability gaps; risk of “initiative fatigue”.
- **Verification Needs:** KPI dashboard (forecast error, stock turns), initiative owners with timelines, override logs for price governance.

### 4.6 Market & Channel Exposure
- **Evidence:** Public channel accounts for 24% of Tech and 46% of Consumer; RAC market −50%; heat pump market −30%; EPBA batteries −7.4%.
- **Implication:** Macro headwinds could undermine recovery; margin compression if RRP hikes resisted.
- **Verification Needs:** Scenario analysis for Public channel mix, price elasticity studies, alternative channel expansion plan.

## 5. Investigation Focus & Diligence Questions

### 5.1 Financial Performance & Margins
- What bridge converts current −€647k YTD EBITDA to breakeven by year-end, and which levers are credible?
- How much of the GP shortfall stems from destocking vs. structural mix? Quantify by BU.
- When does management expect to regain 11% gross margin, and what changes in mix or pricing enable it?
- How is the bottom-price policy monitored, and what exceptions have been approved since September?

### 5.2 Inventory, Working Capital & Cash
- Provide category-level LTS (>120 days) with action owners, margin sacrifice, and burn-down timing.
- What failures in forecasting/purchasing created the Q3 inventory build, and what process controls now exist?
- Deliver a week-by-week cash and covenant forecast covering factoring availability, loan amortization, and inventory payouts.
- Confirm the zinc clearance roadmap (monthly sell-out, third-country sales, April ’25 target).

### 5.3 Business Unit & Commercial Strategy
- Clima & Solar: quantify old vs. new stock GP, and specify the plan to restore >19% margin.
- Consumer: what hurdle rates govern hypermarket expansion, and how will Praktiker GP improve from 3.7%?
- Batteries & Lighting: what is the viability of the business post-clearance? Outline go/no-go criteria.
- Technology: what mix limits reduce Public dependency while preserving growth?

### 5.4 Vendor, Partner & Channel Governance
- Which AUX credit notes are executed vs. pending? Provide amounts, dates, contingencies.
- What minimum zinc purchase obligations bind 2025 rebates, and can they be decoupled from legacy stock clearance?
- How have vendor SLAs, payment terms, or funding lines improved since July?
- Document the PO approval hierarchy introduced in October—limits, overrides, and exceptions granted to date.

### 5.5 Organizational Effectiveness & Transformation
- How is the PM and sales reorganization measured (forecast accuracy, stock turns, GP uplift)?
- What is the timeline for ERP tender selection and go-live risk mitigation?
- Has staff attrition impacted key transformation leaders? What retention safeguards are in place?
- Which July action items remain open (battery reduction, Kydos rebrand, PSI deployment) and who owns each?

### 5.6 Scenario Planning & Risk Management
- Present best/base/worst scenarios for FY24 EBITDA and EBT with triggers for contingency plans.
- Quantify interest-rate sensitivity (±100bps) on the hedged and unhedged debt stack.
- Identify emerging compliance, FX, or supply-chain risks not previously disclosed.
- What early-warning system will flag slippage in inventory or margin targets before month-end?

## 6. Required Evidence & Deliverables Before the Board Meeting
- Monthly PSI files (Purchases-Sales-Inventory) by model with 3-month MAPE and variance commentary.
- Updated inventory aging waterfall with weekly sell-out plan and expected GP impact by category.
- Receivables and payables overdue lists by counterparty, owner, resolution steps, and cash effect.
- Q4 liquidity pack: 13-week cash flow, covenant headroom, factoring utilization, hedge coverage.
- Vendor dossier: status of AUX, Toshiba, Hotpoint agreements with signed documentation and fallback economics.
- Transformation scorecard tracking each initiative from July commitments through current progress, including KPI results and blockers.
- Channel mix analysis detailing Public vs. Other contribution, margin differential, and pricing elasticity tests.
- Governance log summarizing PO approvals since October, overrides, and compliance exceptions.
