## 2. Pre-Meeting Report: Red Flags and Questions

### 2.1 Executive Summary
YTD sales track the revised budget, yet profitability and inventory goals are badly missed. Gross margin erosion, adverse sales mix, and stalled stock reduction have pushed losses beyond full-year targets while debt rises. Immediate corrective action is essential.

### 2.2 Red Flags & Areas Requiring Investigation

#### 2.2.1 Profitability Collapse & Gross Margin Erosion
- **Issue:** GM at 8.4% vs. 9.2% plan; adj. EBITDA −€647k vs. +€162k; EBT −€3.8M.
- **Drivers:** Loss-making destocking in Clima, MDA, Zinc; over-weight reliance on low-margin Technology.

#### 2.2.2 Inventory Management Failure
- **Issue:** Inventory €30.1M (Sep) vs. €26.4M BOY; YE forecast €26.5M.
- **Impact:** −€3.7M operating cash flow, higher debt, further margin pressure from clearance.

#### 2.2.3 Underperformance in Growth Segments
- **Clima & Solar:** Sales −11% vs. budget; GP −44%; old stock GP 3.4% vs. 25.7% on new imports.
- **Consumer Goods & Batteries:** Sales and GP materially below plan; Batteries GP −39% vs. RvBgt.

#### 2.2.4 Business Model Sustainability
- **Issue:** Operational cash burn financed through debt; interest burden rising.
- **Balance Sheet:** Shareholder equity down from €11.0M to €7.2M in nine months.

### 2.3 Board Question Checklist

#### Profitability & Margin
- [ ] Quantify YTD gross profit impact from destocking and forecast Q4 erosion needed for “clean stock” by 2025.
- [ ] Provide evidence that September pricing policy secures minimum profit levels despite declining GP.
- [ ] What share of the €7.5M Clima & Solar inventory is legacy stock, and what is the time-bound liquidation plan without harming 2025 GP?

#### Inventory & Cash Flow
- [ ] Diagnose forecasting/purchasing failures that drove inventory growth and detail process fixes.
- [ ] Present Q4 cash flow forecast and liquidity stress-test, including covenant headroom if Q4 misses occur.

#### Strategy & Execution
- [ ] List urgent Q4 commercial actions to reignite higher-margin Consumer Goods and Batteries & Lighting sales.
- [ ] Report KPIs assessing the PM organization and Consumer department effectiveness; outline contingency steps if Q4 targets fail.
FLAG #3: Critical Underperformance in Key Growth/Profit Segments
Clima & Solar: This segment's performance is alarming. Sales are 11% below budget, but YTD Gross Profit is a staggering 44% below budget. The issue is old stock being sold at a dismal 3.4% GP versus new '24 imports at 25.7% GP.
Consumer Goods & Batteries: Both segments are significantly behind on sales and are contributing far less gross profit than planned, with batteries seeing a YTD Gross Profit 39% below its revised budget.

FLAG #4: Sustainability of the Business Model
The Issue: The current trajectory is unsustainable. The company is funding operational cash burn through increased debt. With rising interest rates, this will continue to erode the bottom line. The balance sheet shows Shareholder's Equity has fallen from €11.0M to €7.2M in just nine months.

3. Specific Questions to Ask in the Meeting
Here are precise, actionable questions to drive accountability and clarify the path forward.
Category 1: On Profitability and Margin
"The 'destocking actions' are the primary reason given for the severe gross margin miss. Can you quantify the total gross profit impact of these actions YTD? How much more margin erosion do you forecast for Q4 to achieve your 'clean stock' goal for 2025?"
"The new pricing policy was implemented in September but GP continued to fall. What concrete evidence do we have that this policy is effective, and how are we reconciling the strategy of 'securing minimum profit levels' with the actual results?"
"Regarding Clima & Solar, what percentage of the €7.5M inventory is considered 'old stock' that needs to be sold at a near-zero margin? What is the specific, time-bound plan to liquidate it without further damaging our brand and our 2025 profitability?"
Category 2: On Inventory and Cash Flow
4. "The plan in July was to reduce inventory, yet it has increased substantially. What were the specific failures in forecasting and purchasing control that led to this outcome? What fundamental changes are being made to the process now to ensure this isn't repeated?"
5. "Given the negative operating cash flow and rising debt to €46.5M, what is the cash flow forecast for Q4? Have we stress-tested our liquidity and bank covenants for a scenario where Q4 sales or margin targets are also missed?"
Category 3: On Strategy and Execution
6. "Our sales mix is dangerously skewed towards low-margin technology. What specific, urgent commercial actions are being taken in Q4 to stimulate sales in the underperforming, higher-margin Consumer Goods and Batteries & Lighting segments?"
7. "The organizational restructure was intended to improve focus and results. Given the YTD performance, how are we measuring the effectiveness of the new PM organization and Consumer department, and are we prepared to make further adjustments if results don't materialize in Q4?"
