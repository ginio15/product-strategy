## 2. Red Flags & Diligence Priorities

### 2.1 Stock & Working Capital
- **Risk:** Chronic LTS with aging batteries/LED and Clima/Solar imbalances.
- **Evidence:** ~70% of LTS >120 days; black split models and zinc clearance unresolved; no inventory reduction July→Nov.
- **Action Needed:** Validate clearance cadence, aging reduction targets, and contingency levers.

### 2.2 Profitability vs Budget
- **Risk:** Margin erosion despite revised budgets.
- **Evidence:** GP under pressure across key BUs; EBITDA stays negative; Batteries & Lighting GP collapse (−38% July → −32% Nov vs budget).
- **Action Needed:** Quantify GP recovery path and confirm pricing/clearance discipline.

### 2.3 Vendor Relations
- **Risk:** Incentives tied to unconfirmed rebates and volume commitments.
- **Evidence:** AUX rebates outstanding; Toshiba rebate linked to zinc purchases; vendor trust recovery unproven.
- **Action Needed:** Obtain written confirmations and outline fallback economics.

### 2.4 Debt & Financing Costs
- **Risk:** Elevated leverage and rising finance costs.
- **Evidence:** Heavy factoring plus €5.5M loan in Sept; expenses up 20–25% YoY; liquidity improvements debt-driven.
- **Action Needed:** Present deleveraging plan, covenant headroom, and interest sensitivity.

### 2.5 Execution Gaps
- **Risk:** Transformation initiatives lack measurable outcomes.
- **Evidence:** “In progress” status repeated since July; PM empowerment/ERP/forecasting not embedded; cultural change impact unclear.
- **Action Needed:** Tie initiatives to KPIs and delivery milestones.

## 3. Board Meeting Question Bank

### 3.1 Financial Performance & Risk
- [ ] Why have destocking and LTS plans failed to reduce inventory since July?
- [ ] What is the cost of carrying old zinc/LED/Clima stock into 2025 (margin + cash)?
- [ ] What is the revised FY2024 break-even EBITDA target post-Q3?

### 3.2 Batteries & LED Crisis
- [ ] What drove further deterioration and what actions go beyond clearance?
- [ ] Does the Toshiba zinc commitment create net value or lock in future risk?

### 3.3 Vendor Relations
- [ ] Can management supply signed AUX rebate and Toshiba agreement evidence?
- [ ] What proves vendor trust has been durably restored?

### 3.4 Debt & Liquidity
- [ ] What is the 2025 deleveraging and refinancing plan?
- [ ] How much 2024 FCF came from factoring/borrowing vs operations?

### 3.5 Execution & Transformation
- [ ] Which initiatives delivered measurable outcomes since July?
- [ ] How has the PM structure improved forecasting, pricing, and vendor negotiations?

### 3.6 Strategic Direction
- [ ] Which business lines should be pruned vs invested in for 2025?
- [ ] What are the top three priorities to restore profitability next year?
