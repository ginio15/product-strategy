## 2. Board Question Bank

### 2.1 Financial Performance & Variances
- [ ] How have the drivers of the YTD EBITDA shortfall evolved since July, and what additional budget adjustments are proposed?
- [ ] Which concrete actions underpin the Scenario 5 €1.6M EBT ambition, especially on overhead and finance cost cuts?

### 2.2 Sales & Segment Progress
- [ ] Why does Batteries & LED Lighting remain −5.6% vs PY, and what timelines exist for initiatives such as the Praktiker partnership or export plays?
- [ ] How will Clima & Solar close the −11.4% budget gap, and what is the Q4 impact of AUX negotiations?

### 2.3 Stock Management & Inventory Risks
- [ ] What tangible progress has been made on excess stock reduction (e.g., Zinc clearance), and how confident are we entering 2025 with clean inventory?
- [ ] With inventory at €30.5M, which controls mitigate aging risk, and how do they align with the €10M reduction objective?

### 2.4 Transformation Initiatives & Organizational Changes
- [ ] Which 2024 roadmap items (ERP tender, commercial policy, etc.) remain on track, and what blockers delay the rest?
- [ ] How are brand repositioning efforts (Dreame launch, social media) translating into measurable sales or margin gains?

### 2.5 Red Flags & Forward-Looking Diligence
- [ ] Which external indicators (e.g., EPBA −7.44% battery market trend) are being tracked, and how do they influence 2025 planning?
- [ ] Are emerging risks in debt/stock management or vendor relationships escalating to require immediate board intervention?
