## 1. Executive Summary of Key Concerns
- **Performance trend:** Between July (June YTD) and September (YTD) presentations, overall profitability continues to deteriorate despite transformation efforts.
- **Cash burn:** EBITDA remains negative (−€647k YTD) and EBT has deepened to −€3.8M.
- **Structural issues:** Margin erosion, bloated inventory, and weak BU execution are creating a feedback loop of destocking losses and rising debt.

## 2. Critical Red Flags

### 2.1 Profitability Crisis Deepening
- July YTD EBITDA: **−€428k** (−0.8% margin).  
- September YTD EBITDA: **−€647k** (−0.7% margin), a **€219k** deterioration.  
- EBT worsened from **−€2.1M** to **−€3.8M**.

### 2.2 Gross Margin Collapse
- September GM: **8.4%** vs. budget **11.1%** (−2.7pp).  
- Destocking impact: **€424k** adverse vs. budget.  
- Clima margin: **12.5%** vs. budget **19.8%** (−7.3pp) — indicates structural pressure.

### 2.3 Inventory Crisis Worsening
- July YE target: **€23.5M**; September actual: **€30.5M** (Dec 2023: €26.4M).  
- Long-term stock (LTS) >1 year: **€6.0M+**; potential YE LTS: **€7.9M** if no action.

### 2.4 Cash Flow Deterioration
- Operating CF (Sept): **+€1.5M**, but driven by receivables, not operations.  
- Loans: **€43.4M** vs. YE target €42M; financial expenses up **24%** YoY to €2.6M.

### 2.5 Business Unit Failures
- **Batteries & Lighting:** Sales −14% vs. revised budget, −5.6% vs. PY; €1.7M revenue shortfall; Toshiba strain; risk of €874k EBITDA impact.  
- **Clima & Solar:** GP margin **12.5%** vs. **18.2%** LY (−5.7pp); AUX credit notes pending; heat pump market −30%.

## 3. Question Bank for the Board Meeting

### 3.1 Profitability & Margins
- [ ] What is the realistic timeline—and probability—of returning to 11% gross margin?
- [ ] Are we prepared to trade revenue growth for margin protection?
- [ ] How much additional destocking impact is forecast for 2025, category by category?
- [ ] What is the monthly bridge from −€647k YTD EBITDA to breakeven?

### 3.2 Batteries & Lighting Crisis
- [ ] Why is the BU still 32% below budget, and is the business model salvageable?
- [ ] Why did the €12.3M plan shrink to €10M, and what is Toshiba’s stance post-September?
- [ ] What is the exit strategy for €2.2M battery stock—are €0.5M third-country sales locked and is April ’25 stable production realistic?
- [ ] Should LED Lighting, 35% below budget with heavy LTS, be restructured or exited?

### 3.3 Inventory & Working Capital
- [ ] Which controls failed, allowing inventory to reach €30.5M vs. €23.5M target?
- [ ] What is the realistic December 2024 closing inventory?
- [ ] How will the €6M–€7.9M LTS be liquidated (actions, timelines, margin cost)?
- [ ] Why have DSO stretched to 89 days, and what is the catch-up plan?

### 3.4 Cash & Debt
- [ ] How will we reduce debt from €43.4M with negative EBITDA, and what YE balance is realistic?
- [ ] Can €2.6M interest costs be reduced meaningfully?
- [ ] When do operations generate positive cash flow without reliance on receivables movements?

### 3.5 Transformation Execution
- [ ] What measurable improvements followed the July PM/Sales reorg (forecast accuracy, stock turns, GP)?
- [ ] Why has LTS not fallen despite Stock Management being priority #1, and who owns the outcome?
- [ ] How can the Kydos profit uplift (9.3% vs. 6.9% YTD) be replicated across categories?

### 3.6 Strategic Relationships
- [ ] With Public at 24% of Tech and 46% of Consumer, what is the dependency risk and mitigation roadmap?
- [ ] Should AUX terms be renegotiated or alternative partners sourced given credit note delays and share targets?
- [ ] What was decided with Toshiba in September—was the 3.7% rebate secured?

### 3.7 Market & Competition
- [ ] Given RAC market decline (−50% GFK), how is Westnet positioned for recovery?
- [ ] How will the 30% heat pump market drop impact the €1.7M inventory sell-through?

### 3.8 Scenarios & Risks
- [ ] What is the probability distribution around the revised −€2.9M EBT plan (best/base/worst)?
- [ ] How will we mitigate the identified €874k EBITDA risk (primarily Batteries/Lighting)?
- [ ] Is a €4M EBITDA run-rate feasible in 2025 to achieve positive EBT, and what levers deliver it?

### 3.9 Governance & Accountability
- [ ] Who is personally accountable for each transformation initiative, and what are the consequences of failure?
- [ ] What is the completion status of July action items (battery stock reduction, old stock clearance, Kydos rebrand, etc.)?
- [ ] What early warning systems will prevent further negative surprises?

## 4. Deep Dive Investigation Areas
- **Customer Concentration Risk:** Top 10 revenue share, payment terms, margin profile, and Public dependency sensitivities.
- **Vendor Relationships:** Renewal timelines and at-risk volumes for HP, Toshiba, AUX; any termination risks.
- **Product Profitability:** SKU-level profit vs. loss and portfolio rationalization opportunities.
- **Competitive Positioning:** Market share trends, win/loss analysis for key deals.
- **Team Stability:** Staff turnover (27% cited), key person risk, transformation leader retention.
- **2025 Budget Assumptions:** Sales growth, margin improvement plan, investment needs.

## 5. Recommended Board Actions

### 5.1 Immediate (This Week)
- Demand a detailed weekly cash-flow forecast for Q4 2024.
- Freeze non-essential purchases until inventory normalizes.
- Require weekly inventory aging dashboards with action plans.
- Commission an independent viability review of Batteries & Lighting.

### 5.2 Short-Term (Next Month)
- Conduct a full strategic review of Batteries & Lighting (fix, sell, or close).
- Renegotiate AUX terms or secure alternative partners.
- Enforce category-level inventory caps with executive accountability.
- Accelerate destocking—even at margin cost—to unlock cash.

### 5.3 Medium-Term (Q1 2025)
- Rationalize the portfolio by exiting unprofitable categories/SKUs.
- Execute a customer diversification strategy to reduce Public dependence.
- Launch a cost-reduction program targeting €1M+ OPEX savings.
- Reassess capital structure sustainability given current debt levels.

## 6. Bottom Line Assessment
- The company is in distress: negative EBITDA, inventory drag, underperforming BUs, and rising debt with operational cash deficits.
- Transformation initiatives have yet to produce tangible financial results.
- The board should shift to crisis management with measurable milestones, enforce accountability, and prepare for tough portfolio and cost decisions.
- **Key question:** Does current management have the urgency and capability to stabilize the business, or is external turnaround support required?
