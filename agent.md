## 2. Qualitative Insights & Red Flags

### 2.1 Underperforming Business Units
- Consumer, Clima & Solar, and Batteries & LED all miss budgets (Consumer −11.7% sales, Clima margin −7.3pp vs. budget, Batteries −32% revenue).
- Investigate demand, pricing, mix, and supply issues; evaluate restructuring or exit options.

### 2.2 Gross Margin Erosion
- Margin down 8.8%→8.4% and far below 11.1% budget due to destocking and price support.
- Quantify intentional margin sacrifice versus structural issues; set recovery timeline.

### 2.3 Persistent Negative EBITDA/EBIT
- Adjusted EBITDA −€0.65M vs. +€1.8M plan.
- Probe why scale failed to deliver profitability, sustainability of losses, and additional cost levers.

### 2.4 Inventory & LTS Exposure
- Stock ~€26.5M with significant LTS and problematic heat pump overstock.
- Need carrying cost analysis, clearance effectiveness, and write-off risk assessment.

### 2.5 Purchase & Planning Controls
- Purchase budget removed; all POs require Oct’24 approvals.
- Confirm approval hierarchy, impact on procurement cadence, and vendor relations.

### 2.6 Transformation Progress
- July initiatives spanned sales/PM restructuring, ERP tender, forecasting, GTM; November notes ongoing stock management and commercial policy updates.
- Document implemented items, realized benefits, delays, and resource needs.

### 2.7 Cash Flow & Debt Transparency
- Finance expense high (~€1.7M YTD June) with limited November disclosure.
- Request net debt, liquidity, covenant status, and refinancing plans.

### 2.8 Market Conditions
- EU portable battery market −7.4% (Toshiba +16%).
- Map macro trends per segment, market share movements, and planned countermeasures.

### 2.9 Profit Improvement Initiatives
- July actions: bottom prices, >48" TV focus, AUX negotiations.
- Validate execution and impact; determine scaling or discontinuation decisions.

## 5. Conclusion
- Sales expansion from June to September did not translate into margin or profit gains.
- Destocking suppressed margins, and multiple BUs lag budgets.
- Emphasis must shift to working-capital reduction, price discipline, and cost control to restore profitability.
- This structure supports rapid assessment and targeted interrogation during the board meeting.

Conclusion
The company achieved sales growth between June and September 2024 but continues to struggle with profitability, margins and inventory management. Several business units significantly underperformed relative to budget, and destocking actions have hurt gross margins. A strong focus on working capital reduction, price and margin management, and disciplined cost control will be essential. The proposed template and questions should help you evaluate the presentations quickly and engage constructively with management during the board meeting.
